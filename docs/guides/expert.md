# Expert guide

For the expert who inspects the car. You use the **Vayu Android app** (the web has the same
**My leads** pages). Your leads are only the ones assigned to you.

## Your leads

The **Leads** tab has Today, Upcoming and Completed. Each lead shows the Booking ID, the car, the
place and the time; tap the phone number to call the customer. A push tells you when a lead is
assigned to you, moved, cancelled, given to someone else, or when its details change.

## Doing an inspection

1. Open the lead, check the summary, press **Start**.
2. **Arrival**: your location and the time are taken by themselves; answer where you came from and
   take the arrival selfie.
3. Go through the steps with **Next** / **Back** (or jump to a step from the list). Every answer
   starts empty. Some questions appear only after an earlier answer.
4. **Photos and videos**: tap the camera for each one, up to the number the question allows. Photos
   taken in the app get a stamp (Booking ID, your name, time, location). You can look at a photo
   full screen, delete it and take it again before you submit.
5. The last steps are the wrap-up (payment questions, Google review), then **Submit**. A review
   screen lists anything required that is still empty; Submit waits until it is filled.

After Submit your answers are locked. The lead shows **Uploading** until every file has reached the
office, then **Completed**. If Ops need a change, they make it; it does not come back to you.

## Photos and uploads

Files upload in the background, on any network, as you go; a broken upload carries on where it
stopped. Keep the app until "Uploading" ends. Files that have not reached the office can never be
cleared from the phone. The app warns when the phone has less than 2 GB free and will not start a
lead below 500 MB.

## Working offline

The app is built to work without a network:

- Your leads and their forms are downloaded in advance; Start, every answer and Submit work offline
  and are sent when the phone is back online.
- Answers are saved on the phone at once. **Until you submit, they exist only on this phone**: if the
  phone is lost before then, the inspection is done again (photos already uploaded are kept).
- The time is corrected from the office's clock at each sync; if the phone has not synced that
  day, Ops see the time marked as unverified.
- If Ops give the lead to someone else while you are working on it, it stays in your list as
  "Reassigned – submit or discard". If you submit, Ops choose which inspection counts.
- You cannot sign out while something is unsent. Signing in on a new phone signs the old one out,
  after the old one has sent its work.

## App updates

When the office needs a newer app, you cannot start new leads until you update (a lead already
started can still be finished). The app offers the download itself.
